🧪 Water Parameters

⚗️ Total Hardness — EDTA Method

Formula:
Hardness = (V × M × 100000) / Vs

Where V = EDTA volume, M = EDTA molarity, and Vs = sample volume.

🔬 Total Alkalinity — Titration

Formula:
Alkalinity = (V × N × 50000) / Vs

Result is expressed as mg/L as CaCO₃.

📖 Chemistry Behind the Analysis

pH

pH = −log₁₀[H⁺]

pH indicates the acidic or basic nature of water.
Hardness

Mainly caused by Ca²⁺ and Mg²⁺ ions.

EDTA forms complexes with these metal ions.
Alkalinity

Mainly related to:
OH⁻, CO₃²⁻ and HCO₃⁻
TDS

Total Dissolved Solids represent dissolved substances such as salts and minerals.
